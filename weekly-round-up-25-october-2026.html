Week ending Sunday 25 October 2026

As the 2026 season winds down, our teams continued their winter programme at Woodlands.

U12 Girls Begin Their Winter League

Our U12 girls continued their football development with a Winter League blitz at Woodlands on Monday evening.

The week also included G4M&O training, boys’ and girls’ underage training and the regular Friday juvenile session. Thank you to every coach, volunteer, parent and player who continues to keep club activity moving as the year draws to a close.
