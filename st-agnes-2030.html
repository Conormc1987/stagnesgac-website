The Future of Our Club

St Agnes 2030

A long-term vision for stronger facilities, more opportunities and a club that can serve Andersonstown for generations to come.

Our Vision

Building the next chapter of St Agnes GAC.

St Agnes has spent 75 years growing through people, families, volunteers and Gaelic games. St Agnes 2030 brings our future plans together in one place: a stronger home for our teams, better opportunities for young people and a community space that reflects the ambition of the club.

The aim is simple: protect what makes St Agnes special while creating the facilities and structures needed for the next generation to thrive.

Our Ambition

More than a playing field.

Our development vision is about sport, community, culture and belonging. We want a home that can serve players from fundamentals to senior level while remaining open and useful to the wider Andersonstown community.

South Link Development

Turning unused land into a lasting community asset.

The South Link project is at the heart of our development plans. The club's ambition is to transform neglected land into a vibrant sporting and community hub with quality playing facilities, modern club infrastructure and space that can be used beyond match day.

Gaelic Games

Improved facilities for football, hurling and camogie across juvenile and adult teams.

Community

A welcoming hub where local people can meet, exercise, learn and connect.

Culture & Heritage

A space that celebrates Irish culture, language, identity and the diverse community around St Agnes.
