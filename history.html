Our Story

History of St Agnes GAC.

From our beginnings in 1951 to the club we are today — generations of players, coaches, families and volunteers have carried St Agnes forward.

Since 1951

Built by generations of Aggies.

St Agnes GAC began life in 1951 and has served the Gaels of Andersonstown for 75 years. The club's story is one of community, friendship, Gaelic games and volunteers who gave their time to make sure each new generation had a place to belong.

This page will become the home for our full club history — including old teams, key milestones, photographs, former players, volunteers and stories from across the decades.

1951 — St Agnes begins

The beginning of a club that would become part of the sporting and community life of Andersonstown.

Growing through the generations

Football, hurling and juvenile development became central to club life as more families became part of St Agnes.

75 years of the Aggies

In 2026 we celebrate 75 years of St Agnes GAC and look forward to building the club for the next generation.
