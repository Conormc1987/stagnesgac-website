U10 Boys Football · Sunday 27 September 2026

A massive day for St Agnes at Woodlands.

Three clubs, brilliant football and a day to remember—despite the weather!

A fantastic U10 tournament.

A massive day for our club as St Agnes welcomed St Oliver Plunkett Eoghan Ruadh from Dublin and St John’s to Woodlands for a fantastic U10 football tournament.

The weather conditions were horrendous, but they did nothing to dampen the enthusiasm of the young players. Every team embraced the conditions, played some brilliant football and helped create a memorable day for everyone involved.

Many thanks to both visiting clubs for joining us at Woodlands and contributing so much to the occasion.

A special word of thanks must also go to all the St Agnes coaches and volunteers whose work made the tournament possible. Their organisation, encouragement and commitment ensured that every child had a brilliant experience.

Well done to all the players and coaches. Aggies Abú! 🖤🤍
