U10 Boys Football · Sunday 27 September 2026

What a day for Naomh Úna!

Five matches. Three clubs. Brilliant football, new friendships and fantastic memories.

A brilliant morning at Páirc Naomh Úna.

What an absolutely brilliant morning at Páirc Naomh Úna as our young lads enjoyed an incredible five matches of football against teams from Naomh Eoin and Oliver Plunketts Eoghan Rua, Dublin.

The football was brilliant, but the day was about so much more than results. It gave our young players the opportunity to test themselves against quality opposition, make new friends, enjoy their football and proudly represent Naomh Úna.

A massive amount of work went on behind the scenes to make the tournament happen. A huge thank you goes to our Juvenile Committee, mentors and everyone who generously gave their time.

Thank you to those who arrived early to set up the pitches, those who refereed the games, everyone who helped throughout the morning and those who kept the tea and coffee flowing for the many parents and visitors. Days like this simply do not happen without volunteers.

We received fantastic feedback from the parents and coaches of both visiting clubs. That is something everyone involved with Naomh Úna should be very proud of.

Our coach Kevin McCann was presented with a commemorative plaque from Oliver Plunketts Eoghan Rua, Áth Cliath—a lovely gesture and a lasting reminder of a very special day between the clubs.

A massive thank you to Naomh Eoin and Oliver Plunketts Eoghan Rua for joining us and helping make the day such a success. Hopefully, this is the beginning of many more occasions like it.

Five matches. Three clubs. Loads of football. New friendships. Brilliant memories.

A fantastic experience for our young lads and a wonderful day for Naomh Úna. Days like this show exactly what our club and the GAA are all about.

Our club. Our young players. Our future. 🖤🤍
Naomh Úna Abú!
