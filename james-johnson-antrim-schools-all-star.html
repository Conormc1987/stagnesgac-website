James Johnson wins Antrim Schools All Star!

A brilliant achievement and a proud moment for James, his family and St Agnes GAC.

Club Pride

An outstanding achievement.

Everyone at St Agnes GAC would like to congratulate James Johnson on receiving an Antrim Schools All Star award.

This recognition is a fantastic achievement for James and reflects the commitment, hard work and talent he continues to show in Gaelic football.

The entire club is extremely proud to see one of our young players recognised at county schools level. Congratulations James — thoroughly deserved, and we look forward to seeing what the future holds.
