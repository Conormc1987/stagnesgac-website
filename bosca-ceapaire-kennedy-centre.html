Bosca Ceapaire opens in the Kennedy Centre

A fantastic new local sandwich deli from two familiar Aggies faces.

Support Local

Congratulations, Cods and Martine!

Everyone at St Agnes GAC would like to congratulate our very own Aggies legends, Cods and Martine, on opening Bosca Ceapaire, their new sandwich deli in the Kennedy Centre.

We wish them every success with this exciting new venture. Call in, say hello and show your support while enjoying their fresh sandwiches, coffee, sweet treats and more.

Best of luck from everyone at St Agnes GAC — we are delighted to see another local business opening its doors in the heart of our community.

Visit Bosca CeapaireBack to Latest News

Bosca Ceapaire is located upstairs in the Kennedy Centre beside Roar and Explore, serving delicious sandwiches and treats.
