50 Years of Service – Roy McLarnon

A remarkable milestone of commitment to Antrim GAA.

Club Pride

Celebrating five decades of dedication.

St Agnes GAC would like to congratulate our own Roy McLarnon on an incredible 50 years of commitment and service to Antrim GAA.

Roy's dedication to Gaelic games over five decades is a remarkable achievement and a testament to the contribution he has made to both club and county.

Everyone at St Agnes is immensely proud to see Roy's years of service recognised. Congratulations Roy — a fantastic milestone and thoroughly deserved.
