Gaelic for Mothers & Others

Football, friendship and a bit of craic.

A welcoming way to get active, meet people and become part of the St Agnes community.

Everyone Welcome

You do not need to have played before.

Gaelic for Mothers & Others is about enjoyment, fitness, friendship and giving women the chance to play Gaelic football in a relaxed and supportive environment. Whether you played years ago or have never kicked a ball, there is a place for you with the Aggies.

Sessions are social, inclusive and built around having fun while learning the game. It is also a brilliant way for parents and families to become more connected with the club.
