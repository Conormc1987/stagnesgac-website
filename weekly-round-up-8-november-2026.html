Club updates from 2–8 November 2026

Girls’ Football · Coláiste Feirste

Valuable Winter League experience for our girls

Our mixed U12 and U14 girls’ team enjoyed a great evening of football at Coláiste Feirste, with plenty of quality play across all three games.

The girls recorded an excellent win over Ardoyne before battling hard to earn another victory against Laochra. Their final game brought a tougher test against O’Donnell’s, but it provided another valuable learning experience for this developing group.

Overall, it was a very positive night. The girls gained more match experience, played some excellent football and represented St Agnes brilliantly throughout. Well done to every player and coach involved. 🖤🤍

U8 & U10 Boys’ Football · Davitts

Young Aggies embrace Friday-night football

Our U8 and U10 boys enjoyed another valuable evening of winter football at Davitts on Friday night.

The lads faced some tough, competitive games and showed plenty of determination, enthusiasm and teamwork throughout. It is fantastic to see our young players continuing to develop and enjoying meaningful matches under the November lights.

Well done to every player who represented the club so proudly, and thanks to the coaches, families and our hosts at Davitts for making the evening possible.
