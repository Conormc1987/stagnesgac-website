U14 Championship · 3 October 2026

U14 lads do St Agnes proud in championship thriller

Naomh Úna 5-7 · Naomh Pól 5-10

Well done to our U14 lads who played out an absolute thriller against St Paul’s today in the Championship. The lads did St Agnes proud, battling right to the final whistle in a brilliant contest. Their improvement over the course of the year has been phenomenal, and they can take huge pride in how far they’ve come as a team.

A great group of young players with plenty more to come. We’ll now build towards 2027 together. Maith sibh, lads!
